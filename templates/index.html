About Landslide AI

Problem

Landslides can occur due to heavy rainfall, soil saturation, slope instability and terrain conditions. Early detection and rapid communication can help communities respond to increasing risk.

Solution

LANDSLIDE AI combines machine learning, environmental monitoring, explainable risk analysis, impact assessment and resilient communication into one system.

Key Innovation

The system does not stop at prediction. It connects:

AI Prediction → Explanation → Escalation → Impact → Warning → Last-Mile Communication
